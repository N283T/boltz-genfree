WebGPU tool

Screen in the browser

Rank ligands against one receptor on your own GPU. Nothing is uploaded.

Paste a receptor and a list of ligands, keep or adjust the settings, and run. Each ligand gets a GenFree score; the table ranks them and saves as scores.csv.
