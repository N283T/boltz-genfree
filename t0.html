Ranking a library without building a structure for every candidate

Structure-generation-free ranking for first-stage affinity screening

Tsubasa Nagae1,2 and Kentaro Tomii1,2,*

1Graduate School of Medical Life Science, Yokohama City University, Yokohama, Japan  ·  2AIRC, National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), Tokyo, Japan  ·  *k-tomii@aist.go.jp

Code Data and analysis Resources

Boltz-2 scores affinity by co-folding a structure for every candidate. GenFree skips structure generation, keeps the released affinity checkpoint unchanged, and returns only a ranking.
