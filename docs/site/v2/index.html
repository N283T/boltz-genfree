Ranking a library without building a structure for every candidate

Structure-generation-free ranking for first-stage affinity screening

Boltz-2 scores affinity by co-folding a structure for every candidate. GenFree builds none, keeps the released affinity checkpoint unchanged, and returns only a ranking.

At the same time, several studies suggest that the pose contributes little to the predicted affinity (Ujihara 2026; Bret et al. 2026; Furui and Ohue 2025).

We examined this in detail by intervening on the individual inputs of the affinity module.

Based on those results, we built GenFree. It uses the same Boltz-2 weights, generates no structure, and screens fast.

How the Boltz-2 affinity prediction uses a structure

A Boltz-2 affinity run hands the affinity module two things that depend on the generated structures: the token set it scores, and a binned distogram.

Two structures are generated: stage 1 co-folds the complex, and stage 2 samples local poses again on the crop.

(a) The crop: which tokens the affinity checkpoint sees

The cropper orders tokens by distance to the ligand and adds chain stretches until a limit: 256 tokens, 200 protein tokens, 2,048 atoms. The checkpoint sees the ligand and its neighbourhood, not the receptor.

The crop decides what the network conditions on, and Section 4 shows it moves the result.

(b) The distogram: how the sampled coordinates enter the score

The affinity checkpoint does not reuse the first pose. It samples its own local poses on the crop and keeps the highest-ipTM one. Those coordinates enter only as pairwise distances in 64 bins, boundaries 2 to 22 Å, through a learned embedding.

03

What earlier work already suggested

Three independent readings point the same way: the pose the affinity module is handed matters much less than the context around it.

Boltzina docks each ligand with Vina into a grid from one reference complex per target, then uses the released affinity pathway. Two-stage designs exist too, rescoring a top 20% or a top 10% with Boltz-2.

New here 1

No structure at all

Earlier two-stage designs need docked poses or a full Boltz-2 pass first. GenFree needs no structure for any candidate.

New here 2

Interventions on the retained inputs

Earlier tracing asked how far a pose moves the value. Here the question is which remaining inputs the ranking can lose.

New here 3

Full libraries, not panels

Eight full libraries of about 50,000 compounds, against the released Boltz-2 and Boltzina scores.

Crop-construction sensitivity

05

What GenFree replaces, and the two routes it offers

GenFree keeps the released affinity trunk and heads and swaps exactly the two inputs that depend on structure generation: the token set and the distogram.

The context-supplied route keeps the external context a target had: a reference crop, an MSA, two-dimensional ligand positions. Cutting the crop is the costly step; the two targets without an experimental complex use one structure predicted per target.

The context-free route passes the complete receptor, a query-only MSA and zero ligand positions. No per-target preparation, and lower AP on all eight targets.

Affinity recycling is zero in both, against five in the standard pipeline: on 128-compound subsets of five targets more passes did not improve AP.

06

Results on eight full libraries

On its own GenFree keeps 90% of the Boltz-2 mean AP, against 67% for Boltzina. How much survives depends on the target and the endpoint.

The evidence is the eight MF-PCBA libraries of the Boltzina benchmark, eight of the ten assays Boltz-2 held out. The context-supplied route is carried forward: higher AP on all eight targets than the context-free one.

Against Boltzina, AP and AUROC agree target for target: above it on ALR, GSK3A and GSK3B, by +0.0962 in AP on GSK3B, below it on CTDSP1 by −0.0055.

Against Boltz-2 the picture is mixed. NSD2, CTDSP1, NS5 and NS1 lose both endpoints, NS5 by the most; GSK3B gains. AUROC retains least and AP retains most.

A continuous-affinity check

Ranking a library and predicting pX in a congeneric series are different tasks. On the four FEP+ series Boltz-2 reports, the mean corrected Pearson correlation is 0.729 for the generated control, 0.726 for the reference-crop candidate and 0.707 for the full-receptor one.

Per series the full-receptor candidate falls below the control on CDK2, JNK1 and p38 by −0.047, −0.085 and −0.140, and rises on TYK2 by +0.185. The ligand-only control reaches 0.312.

07

The two-stage screen: GenFree first, Boltz-2 on the top 10%

Re-ranking only the top 10% of the GenFree ranking with the published Boltz-2 score reaches the eight-library mean AP of Boltz-2 alone, 0.0839 against 0.0840, at an estimated 8.5 to 8.9 times lower cost.

A first stage ranks the library; its top 5, 10 or 20%, fixed in advance, is re-ordered by a published Boltz-2 score, and the rest keeps its first-stage order. GenFree and Boltzina are compared on the same libraries.

Averaged over the eight targets the top-10% GenFree screen matches Boltz-2 alone, and GenFree alone does not. With Boltzina first the construction stays below Boltz-2 at every share.

The recovery sits where GenFree alone falls short: on NSD2, CTDSP1, NS5 and NS1 the top 20% lifts AP by +0.0081 to +0.0855. Where GenFree leads, the second stage costs AP. The best share depends on the library.

Cost

Cost is one first-stage pass over the library plus one Boltz-2 pass over the shortlist, from the per-compound times of the two timed targets. At 10% that is 8.5 and 8.9 times lower than Boltz-2 alone, against 3.3 and 1.9 with Boltzina first.

The second released Boltz-2 score

The second released score reads the same, with two differences: at a 5% shortlist the mean difference from Boltz-2 alone is −0.0044, and NS1 stays below it at 20%.

Why a short list is enough

The two rankings share a mean 0.42 of their top 10% against 0.10 by chance, yet a mean 0.83 of the actives Boltz-2 puts in its own top 1% lie inside the GenFree top 10%, and 0.93 inside the top 20%.

08

The cheap ranking can also choose which compounds to label

Fine-tuning the affinity modules on 300 GenFree-selected labels per target raises AP on 7 of the 8 targets, while AUROC rises on only 3.

The top 300 compounds of the completed context-supplied screen form the training pool, with labels read only after selection. Both affinity modules train with a binary focal loss and AdamW, the trunk frozen, for five epochs.

AUROC rises on only 3 targets and falls on 4; NSD2 drops from 0.7645 to 0.6148. Top-100 recovery goes from 16 to 50 on NS5 and from 25 to 58 on GSK3B, and is unchanged on NSD2 and FadD28.

Is a GenFree-chosen label set as good as a Boltz-2-chosen one? On the top 300 of a Boltz-2 ranking fine-tuned GenFree reaches a mean AP of 0.1306, up from 0.0558, against the 0.1340 reported for fine-tuning ordinary Boltz-2.

Fine-tuned GenFree against fine-tuned Boltz-2

On the same 300 Boltz-selected labels the two reach 0.1306 (GenFree) and 0.1340 (Boltz-2) and rank different compounds: mean rank correlation 0.46, top-10% overlap 0.39. Averaging the two ranks gives 0.1520, but that needs both models on every compound.

Selecting with GenFree and then fine-tuning ordinary Boltz-2 needs new Boltz-2 inference, and is left for future work.
09

Limitations

What this evidence does not establish.

  • Accuracy varies widely by target for every route here, Boltz-2 and Boltzina included.
  • Transfer is untested. The eight targets were chosen before the analysis, and the panels that shaped the inputs come from the same libraries.
  • Inherited assumptions. Boltz-2's leakage control for these assays is protein-level, and its affinity training set is not public.
  • Labels cost something: training and label acquisition sit outside the runtime ratios.
  • No pose. A compound carried forward still needs a structure built for it, at shortlist scale.

Notes

  • Published Boltz-2 and Boltzina scores are compound-ID-aligned references, not matched reruns.
  • The two-stage screen is simulated from released scores, not run end to end.
  • The NS5 Boltzina run returned 2,030 of 2,048 scores.
  • Mechanisms are hypotheses; the interventions are diagnostics inside a generated context.
  • One checkpoint flag is unconfirmed: whether the stage-2 trunk runs without templates.
  • Conditions B and C read the generated complex and stay diagnostics.
  • Intervals are exploratory and unadjusted; counts and correlations are point estimates.
  • The token strip in Section 2 is schematic.

Methods, settings, install and citation

Every number on this page comes from the curated tables of the archived analysis.

Evaluation populations and targets

The eight MF-PCBA libraries are eight of the ten assays Boltz-2 held out, with proteins at least 90% identical excluded from its affinity training. Boltzina left out two of the ten. Recorded target_active_v2 labels are used unchanged.

Conditions use enriched panels of 512 compounds, 128 positive and 384 negative, drawn deterministically. The conditioning study uses 128-compound subsets (32 positives).

Recorded model settings

Condition study. Seed 20260714, affinity recycling five, 200 steps and five samples declared, enabled kernels, no molecular-weight correction, runtime 2.10.10.

Full-library GenFree. Seed 20260718, released affinity checkpoint, affinity recycling zero, batch eight, enabled kernels, folded conditioning, no molecular-weight correction. Context-supplied: at most 1,024 MSA sequences, RDKit two-dimensional ligand positions, supplied reference crops except on ALR, NSD2 and CTDSP1. Context-free: full receptor, zero ligand positions, query-only MSA input.

Implementation. A library is scored in resumable pieces. Folded conditioning is an algebraic rearrangement and moves scores by at most 2.5 × 10⁻⁶.

Timing. One GPU and 16 logical CPUs per method and target on ABCI rt_HG. Boltz-2: official 2.2.1, supplied MSA, seed 20260908, structure recycling three, 200 steps, one sample; affinity recycling five, 200 steps, five samples. GenFree: seed 20260718, recycling zero, batch eight, folded conditioning, external reference crop, MSA subsampling, RDKit two-dimensional positions. Boltzina: v1.0.1, Vina 1.2.7, 20 Å box, exhaustiveness eight, 16 docking workers, affinity batch eight, recycling five, seed 20260908.

Fine-tuning. Both affinity modules train on detached contexts, trunk frozen. Focal losses are summed with α₁ = 0.8, α₂ = 0.6 and γ = 2. AdamW: learning rate 2 × 10⁻⁵, (β₁, β₂) = (0.9, 0.95), epsilon 10⁻⁸, no weight decay, clipping ten. Five epochs, batch one, accumulation four give 375 optimizer steps; learning seed zero gives 1,500 draws per 300-record pool. FP32 on detached BF16 contexts.

Metrics and intervals

The aggregate binary probability, the mean of the two affinity modules, is ranked high to low and read with AUROC, average precision and enrichment at the top 1% and 5%. AP is non-interpolated; ties are grouped, and boundary ties are shared in proportion.

Panel and seed contrasts use 1,000-replicate label-stratified paired compound bootstrap intervals; full-library and FT300 contrasts use 2,000. The molecular-weight correction is Boltz-2's own, 1.035 v − 0.600 MW0.3 + 2.833.

Does the score depend on the target at all?

4,377 compounds appear in two libraries with opposite recorded labels. The context-supplied route puts the higher score on the target where the compound is active for 64.7% of them [63.2, 66.1], against 62.1% for published Boltz-2 and 62.5% for published Boltzina.

Scores on shared compounds correlate across target pairs at a median Spearman of 0.617 for the context-supplied route and 0.588 for the reference.

Install and usage

Python 3.10 or newer, installed from a tagged release. The affinity checkpoint downloads on first use.

git clone https://github.com/N283T/boltz-genfree.git
cd boltz-genfree
uv sync --frozen --extra cuda      # --extra cuda12 for CUDA 12 PyTorch builds
source .venv/bin/activate

A screen config takes one protein source and one ligand source.

version: 1

target:
  sequence: YOUR_PROTEIN_SEQUENCE
  msa: ./target.a3m          # or auto / empty
  crop:                      # optional when the complete receptor fits
    reference: ./reference-complex.cif
    reference_protein: A
    reference_ligand: L

ligands:
  csv: ./library.csv
  id_column: id
  smiles_column: smiles

output_dir: ./screen-output
boltz-genfree screen screen.yaml --prepare-only   # dry run: validate the inputs
boltz-genfree screen screen.yaml                  # run; scores are written to scores.csv

A re-run with the same inputs resumes. Options are documented in docs/usage.md and docs/finetuning.md.
